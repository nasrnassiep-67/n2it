## Request for the voip-server session: company contacts endpoint (2026-10-06)
The iOS and Android apps now show a "Company" section in Contacts from FusionPBX > Apps > Contacts. They need
this endpoint on the PBX (until it exists they get 404 and show only phone contacts):

- `GET https://<tenant>.voip.n2it.co.za/app/n2it/contacts.php`
- Auth: HTTP Basic, username = extension (e.g. `1002`), password = that extension's SIP password. Resolve the
  domain from the Host header, check the extension exists, is enabled and the password matches; else `401`.
  Rate-limit failed attempts (fail2ban or similar), since it accepts SIP passwords.

- `200`, `Content-Type: application/json`:
  `{"contacts":[{"name":"Acme Ltd","numbers":[{"label":"Work","number":"0211234567"}]}]}`
  `name`: the contact's display name, or organisation if no person's name. Skip contacts with no numbers.

Reply from the voip-server session (2026-10-06): built as specified in `/var/www/n2it-api/directory.php`, served at
`/app/n2it/contacts.php` (nginx exact-match route, 30 req/min per IP, burst 10; failed logins logged as

the tenant's extensions come first in `contacts`, one number each with label `Extension` (n2it's two FusionPBX

4. Open `N2IT.xcodeproj`. Signing is preset in `project.yml` (team 8URJCQ48GM, automatic); check Signing &
   Capabilities shows that team with no errors.
5. Plug in the iPhone (Developer Mode on), run. Test: call 1002 <-> another extension, then Add participant,
   Merge calls, hang up.
6. Commit fixes on a branch (e.g. `ios-fixes`) and push; the voip-server session reviews and merges to main.

Rules: no secrets in the repo; do not change the PBX from the Mac (that is done from the voip server).

## iOS 0.2.3: re-register on resume (Mac session, 2026-10-12, branch `ios-register-resume`)
Reply to "1002 iPhone does not ring". iOS suspends the app a few seconds after it leaves the screen or the phone
locks, and the TLS connection to the PBX dies with it. Back on screen, Linphone still thought it was registered and
only re-registered at the next 10 min refresh, so the PBX had no live contact. 0.2.3 calls `enterForeground()` +
`refreshRegisters()` every time the app comes back on screen (and `enterBackground()` when it leaves), so a fresh
REGISTER should show on the PBX within seconds of opening the app. Installed on the owner's iPhone 17.
While the app is in the background or the phone is locked, iOS will not keep a SIP connection open: the iPhone can
only ring then through VoIP push (APNs key + enable-push.sh 1002, still parked). Team ID is now 8URJCQ48GM (PR #13).

**Why.** Belledonne's closed-source licence for Liblinphone costs €16,200 (iOS only) to €19,800 (iOS + Android), plus

the calling engine natively (owner chose native over Flutter) on WebRTC (BSD) + our own SIP client. The screens,
CallKit, push and accounts stay. Ebrahim confirmed in writing that N2IT owns all app code he wrote. Linphone builds
already handed out (Android <= 0.2.14, the current iOS TestFlight/Ad Hoc build) still owe their source to the people who
got them: pending owner decision. Never submit a Linphone build to the App Store.

**How Android 0.3.0 works** (copy this design to Swift when told to):
- `SipStack.kt`: SIP over a secure WebSocket, `wss://<company>.voip.n2it.co.za/wss` on port 443 (nginx passes it to
  FreeSWITCH's WSS listener, the same path the desktop JsSIP app uses). Subprotocol `sip`; Via `SIP/2.0/WSS <rnd>.invalid`;
  Contact `<sip:<rnd>@<rnd>.invalid;transport=ws>`; REGISTER every 600 s; digest MD5 for 401/407; WebSocket pings every
  25 s (the PBX answers them, checked). A wrong password is not retried (fail2ban).
- `CallMedia.kt`: one WebRTC PeerConnection per call. All ICE candidates go in the SDP (FreeSWITCH does not do trickle
  ICE): wait until gathering completes, or 1 s with no new candidate, 4 s at most. DTMF = RFC 2833 via the sender's
  DtmfSender. Hold = our transceiver direction sendonly + re-INVITE.
- `SipManager.kt`: calls, hold, blind transfer (REFER), attended transfer (REFER with Replaces: to-tag = our remote tag,
  from-tag = our local tag), voicemail lamp (SUBSCRIBE message-summary), DND (486), decline (603), call waiting.
- No 3-way calls yet: next is a conference room on the PBX (calls REFERred into it) instead of mixing on the phone.

**What FreeSWITCH offers a WebSocket client** (Local SDP, 2026-10-22): `RTP/SAVPF` G722, PCMU, PCMA, telephone-event 101,
CN; DTLS `a=setup:actpass` + sha-256 fingerprint; `a=rtcp-mux`; one host candidate 197.189.233.254; old-style SSRC/msid
lines; **no `a=mid` and no `a=group:BUNDLE`**. No Opus (mod_opus is not loaded), so calls run G.722 to the PBX, which
converts to G.729/G.711 for the trunks.

**Bugs found while testing on Android 0.3.0, avoid them on iOS:**
1. `bundlePolicy = maxBundle` makes WebRTC refuse every FreeSWITCH offer ("max-bundle configured but session description
   has no BUNDLE group", 488 on answer). Use **balanced** (the browser default).
2. Missing `a=mid`: the app adds `a=mid:0…` per m= line before applying the PBX's SDP (harmless, kept).
3. Add the microphone with **`addTrack`**, not `addTransceiver`. A transceiver from addTransceiver is not reused for the
   PBX's offer, so the answer said `a=recvonly` and sent no audio (answered call, no audio, 12:19).
4. Never send an answer without ICE candidates. Once WebRTC reported gathering complete with none: the answer had port 9 and no
   candidates, and the PBX hung up at once (INCOMPATIBLE_DESTINATION). Now: wait for at least one, else fail with a reason.
5. Echo: with the phone's **built-in** echo canceller (HONOR) a mobile caller heard herself on our call. Default is now
   WebRTC's software canceller, with a Settings switch "Use the phone's own echo canceller" (default off). On iOS use
   WebRTC's normal voice-processing audio unit (it has Apple's echo canceller); retest echo on speaker.
6. Android needed ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (Linphone used to add it): crash on start without it.
7. Make errors visible: the app shows WebRTC's exact error (toast + Settings > Last call problem > Copy details). That
   turned guesswork into one-build fixes. Do the same on iOS.

**Tested on Android 0.3.0 (owner's HONOR, ext 1000 @ n2it, 2026-10-22):** internal calls both ways ✅; outside calls both
ways incl. MTN 073 908 0355 ✅; Bluetooth earbuds connected mid-call, audio moved over ✅; call pickup `**1000` ✅;

## 3-way calls without Linphone: PBX conference room, Android 0.3.1 (voip-server session, 2026-10-22, branch `android-conference`)
Still: **no iOS work until the owner says go.** This is how 3-way calls work once Linphone is gone; copy it when told.

- Linphone mixed a conference on the phone. WebRTC can't do that easily, so the **PBX mixes it**: FusionPBX's standard
  global dialplan **`nb-conference`**: dialling `*30` + 2-7 digits joins room `<digits>@<company domain>` (conference
  profile `default`). It exists for every tenant, so **no PBX change is needed**. Proven on n2it 2026-10-22: a test join
  made room `9876543@n2it.voip.n2it.co.za`.
- **Merge calls** (one caller held, one answered): the app calls a new random room `*30` + 7 digits, and once that call
  is up it **blind-transfers (REFER) each person into the room** (`Refer-To: <sip:*30NNNNNNN@<domain>>`). The PBX ends our
  legs to them; the app keeps one call, to the room. The conference screen shows the people we moved in (we keep that
  list ourselves; the PBX does not tell the phone who is in the room).
- **Add participant during a conference**: ring the new person **without holding the room**. Holding it would play hold
  music to everyone in it, so only our microphone is muted on the room call. When they answer, REFER them into the room
  and unmute.
- **Differences from Linphone**: people in the room can't be removed one by one from the phone; they leave by hanging up
  (someone still ringing can be dropped). When we hang up, the others stay connected to each other.
- Android code: `SipManager.kt` (`merge`, `addParticipant`, `moveIntoRoom`, `confCall`/`confRoom`/`confPeople`).
- Status: built as Android 0.3.1, **being tested** (not on `main`/download page yet). Results will follow here.
